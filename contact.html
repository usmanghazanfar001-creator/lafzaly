Contact us

We read every message. The fastest way to reach us is email.

Email: YOUR-EMAIL@YOUR-DOMAIN.com

What to write to us about

  • Support. Something not working? Tell us what you did, what you expected, and what happened. Include the file type and approximate length for video problems. Screenshots help.
  • Billing. Questions about Lafzaly Pro, receipts or cancelling a subscription. Please write from the email address on your account.
  • Privacy requests. To access, correct or delete your account data, write from your account email. See our Privacy Policy.
  • Feedback and ideas. Tell us which words, spellings or phrases the Roman Urdu output gets wrong. This feedback directly improves the product.

Before you write

Many questions are answered in the FAQ and the guides. We aim to reply as soon as we can, but we are a small team, so replies may take a few days.
