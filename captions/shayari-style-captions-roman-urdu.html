Common questions

Can I copy these shayari-style captions for my own posts?

Yes. Tap any caption to copy it, then paste it into Instagram, TikTok, Facebook or WhatsApp. You can change a word or two so it sounds like you.

How do I write a shayari-style caption in Roman Urdu myself?

Keep it short, write the way you speak, and spell each word the same way every time. Lafzaly's free caption generator can write fresh options for your exact topic if you want something new.

What are the best hashtags to use with Roman Urdu captions?

Mix one broad tag like #RomanUrdu or #Pakistan with two or three specific ones for your topic and city. Five or six hashtags is plenty.
